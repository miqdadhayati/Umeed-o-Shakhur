Our first camp brought students together for a week of structured sessions run by volunteer mentors. Days moved between classroom teaching, small-group work, and time outdoors.

Sessions covered university and career pathways, health and personal hygiene, climate and environment, and practical study skills. Students worked in small groups on shared projects, presented back to the room, and closed the week with a certificate ceremony.

The camp was hosted in a local school building, with sessions spilling outdoors when the weather allowed.
