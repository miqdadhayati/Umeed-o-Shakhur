Our second camp ran on a simpler footprint: mats under the trees instead of a classroom, with mentors teaching in circles and students working together on chart paper.

Alongside the teaching sessions, students took what they had learned into the village — presenting their work to families in their own homes. The week closed with a certificate ceremony for everyone who took part.
